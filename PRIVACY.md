# Lightmorphic Paste — privacy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Lightmorphic Paste does not collect anything.

## What it stores, and where

The notes you save are kept as ordinary bookmarks in your own browser, in a
folder named Lightmorphic Paste. A few small preferences — your chosen colour, light
or dark, which tab you had open — are kept in the browser's own extension
storage. That is all of it.

Nothing is sent anywhere. The extension makes no network requests of any kind.
There is no server, no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no
tracking. Nobody but you ever sees your notes.

If you have your browser's sync turned on, your notes travel between your own
computers the same way the rest of your bookmarks do. That is your browser's
sync, run by whoever makes your browser, and Lightmorphic Paste is not part of it.

## Why it asks for each permission

- **Bookmarks** — the notes *are* bookmarks. It reads and writes only inside its
  own folder.
- **Storage** — remembers your colour, light or dark, and which tab you were on.
- **Context menus** — puts "Add to Lightmorphic Paste" on the right-click menu when you
  have text selected, and lists your notes when you right-click a text box.
- **Active tab** and **scripting** — used only at the moment you ask for a paste,
  to place text into the box you were typing in. Nothing is read from the page,
  and nothing runs on a page until you press a shortcut or click a menu item.
- **Clipboard write** — copies a note when you click it.

## What that means for you

Because your notes are bookmarks, they are as visible as any other bookmark.
Anyone who can open your browser can read them, and they travel wherever your
bookmark sync goes. Lightmorphic Paste says so the first time you open it, and again
on its settings page.

Keep passwords, card numbers and anything else private out of it, and use a
password manager for those instead.

## Removing it

Uninstalling the extension leaves your notes behind, because they are bookmarks.
Delete the Lightmorphic Paste bookmark folder and they are gone for good.
