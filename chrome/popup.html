Before you start

Everything you save here is kept as a bookmark in your browser. That is what lets your notes follow you between computers with no account, no server and nothing to sign up for.

It also means your notes are as visible as any other bookmark. Anyone who can open your browser can read them, and they travel wherever your bookmark sync goes.

So keep passwords, card numbers, and anything else private out of Pastemorphic. Use a password manager for those.
