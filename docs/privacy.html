Privacy

Lightmorphic Paste does not collect anything. There is nowhere for it to send anything to.

Last updated 2 September 2026

What it stores, and where

The notes you save are kept as ordinary bookmarks in your own browser, in a folder named Lightmorphic Paste. A few small preferences are kept in the browser's own extension storage: your chosen accent colour, which tab you had open, and whether a click should paste as well as copy. That is all of it.

Nothing is sent anywhere. The extension makes no network requests of any kind. There is no server, no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nobody but you ever sees your notes.

If you have your browser's sync turned on, your notes travel between your own computers the same way the rest of your bookmarks do. That is your browser's sync, run by whoever makes your browser, and Lightmorphic Paste is not part of it.

Why it asks for each permission

  • Bookmarks. The notes are bookmarks. It reads and writes only inside its own folder.
  • Storage. Remembers your accent colour, which tab you were on, and whether a click pastes as well as copies.
  • Context menus. Puts "Add to Lightmorphic Paste" on the right-click menu when you have text selected, and lists your notes when you right-click a text box.
  • Active tab and scripting. Used only at the moment you ask for a paste, to place text into the box you were typing in. Nothing is read from the page, and nothing runs on a page until you press a shortcut or click a menu item.
  • Clipboard write. Copies a note when you click it.

What that means for you

Because your notes are bookmarks, they are as visible as any other bookmark. Anyone who can open your browser can read them, and they travel wherever your bookmark sync goes. Lightmorphic Paste says so the first time you open it, and again on its settings page.

Keep passwords, card numbers and anything else private out of it, and use a password manager for those instead.

Removing it

Uninstalling the extension leaves your notes behind, because they are bookmarks. Delete the Lightmorphic Paste bookmark folder and they are gone for good.
