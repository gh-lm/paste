Cookies

This site sets no cookies. Nothing about you is tracked here, and nothing is passed to anyone else.

What is not here

There is no analytics, no advertising, no social buttons, no embedded video, and no fonts or scripts loaded from anywhere else. Every file the page needs comes from this site. That means no third party learns you visited, because no third party is involved.

The one thing that is stored

If you press the light and dark button in the corner, your choice is kept in your own browser under the name pm-theme. It holds one word, either light or dark.

  • It is not a cookie. It never leaves your browser and is never sent with a request, so this site cannot read it and neither can anyone else.
  • Nothing is stored until you actually press the button. Arrive, read, and leave without touching it and nothing is written at all.
  • It cannot identify you. It is one word about how you like a page to look.
  • Clearing your browser's site data removes it, and the page goes back to following whatever your device is set to.

The extension

Lightmorphic Paste, the extension itself, is a separate thing from this website and also sets no cookies and makes no network requests. The privacy page covers what it stores and where.
