Cookies

This site sets no cookies. Nothing about you is tracked here, and nothing is passed to anyone else.

What is not here

There is no analytics, no advertising, no social buttons, no embedded video, and no fonts or scripts loaded from anywhere else. Every file the page needs comes from this site. That means no third party learns you visited, because no third party is involved.

Nothing is stored either

The site keeps nothing in your browser at all. No cookies, no local storage, no session storage. Close the tab and there is no trace that you were here, because there never was one to leave.

It runs no JavaScript of its own. The pages are plain HTML and one stylesheet.

The extension

Lightmorphic Paste, the extension itself, is a separate thing from this website and also sets no cookies and makes no network requests. The privacy page covers what it stores and where.

Asking about this

Write to privacy@lightmorphic.com.
